2 · Answer delivered

What sarah.koehler saw
Copy answer
Under ERISA § 408, certain transactions that would otherwise be prohibited under § 406 are exempt. For plan advisors, the most relevant is DOL PTE 2020-02, which lets investment advice fiduciaries receive otherwise prohibited compensation if they meet Impartial Conduct Standards and make the required disclosures. ERISA § 408(b)(2) also permits reasonable compensation to service providers for necessary services. PTE 84-14 (QPAM) may apply to qualified plan asset managers, but its investment advice language is partly superseded by PTE 2020-02. This conflict is flagged for your review.
